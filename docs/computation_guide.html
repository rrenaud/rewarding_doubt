Three ways to reward doubt

The released Rewarding Doubt baseline and our two variants train the same thing: the confidence a model states after its answer. They share every step up to the point where that confidence gets scored. This guide shows where they split, how the variants get every confidence level's probability from a single forward pass, what each method computes for one question, and what each costs.

Released PPO (baseline) Discrete-exact Fractional

Where the computation splits

Every method runs the released code's front half unchanged. The current model reads a TriviaQA question and samples Answer: …, Confidence at temperature 0.6, stopping at the token Confidence. The released F1 grader labels the answer correct (y = 1) or wrong (y = 0). Then each method samples one confidence string at temperature 1, as the released code's second generate call does. What they do with that sample is the difference.

Where the 11 probabilities come from

The variants teacher-force one sequence: the prompt and answer, then :, a space and the sampled number. In Llama-3 every confidence level from 0 to 10 is a single token, so the model's output at the position after the space is one softmax that already contains all eleven of them. The output one position later says whether the model would stop after the sampled number.

Checked against scoring all 11 complete strings. On real prompts, for the base model and a trained discrete-exact checkpoint:

  • The model stops after the number with probability 0.9995–0.9998 averaged over levels weighted by π, on both models. The direct stop check at the most likely level read at least 0.9995. The lowest single-level estimate was 0.95 on the trained checkpoint, but that estimate is a difference of two noisy bf16 log-probabilities.
  • Dropping the stop token changes π by at most 0.008 nats of KL. The largest shift in any πk (0.06) equals the shift between two bf16 computations of the very same quantity.
  • The gradient matches the 11-string computation as closely as that computation matches itself (cosine 0.997–0.999).
  • Forward plus backward takes 0.26 s per question, against 0.67 s for 11 full strings.

What one step costs

Each square is one forward pass over one question's sequence, about 160 tokens. At this length a pass is dominated by reading the 4-bit weights, not by the token count, so the number of passes is what sets the cost.

In practice

  • Choose PPO when the output space is open. It needs only a reward for whatever the model wrote, which is why the paper uses it, and it handles format errors without extra terms.
  • Choose an exact variant when the confidence comes from a short list of single tokens. At the same number of updates it calibrated as well as the released PPO and ranked answers better, without a reference model, value head or KL tuning.
  • Give the exact variants the same number of updates. With one update per batch they trailed PPO on AUROC; the gap closed and reversed at 8.
  • Discrete-exact is the steadier of the two. Fractional reached the lowest ECE early (0.031) but drifted up to 0.099 before recovering; discrete-exact improved almost monotonically.
  • Discrete-exact vs fractional is also a choice about the output. Discrete-exact commits to one level, so a sampled reply prints the calibrated number. Fractional's target is the mean, best read from the 11 probabilities.

Symbols

π_k
Probability of confidence level k: the softmax at the position before the number, restricted to the 11 number tokens and renormalized
k*
The confidence number the model sampled at temperature 1 this step; used for the stop check
R(p, y)
Clipped log score, y·log p + (1 − y)·log(1 − p), rescaled to [−1, 1]. The released code uses a ×10 scale with a +2.5 bonus for correct answers.
β
PPO's KL coefficient: 0.05 at the start, adapted toward 6 nats per sequence
GAE
Generalized advantage estimation: the reward minus the value head's prediction, carried back over the response tokens (γ = 1, λ = 0.95)
hinges
1.0 × relu(log 0.95 − log M), where M is the probability of ": " followed by any number, plus the same form on P(<eot> | k*). Both are zero while the format holds.
