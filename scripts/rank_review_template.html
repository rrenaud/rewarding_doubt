Rewarding Doubt · Llama-3-8B · LoRA on layers 16–31

Rank 4 or rank 8?

Every run we have that compares LoRA ranks with everything else equal, what each shows, and how much weight it can bear.

Verdict: rank 4 is the better default, but the evidence is thin.

Offline (2 seeds), rank 4 calibrates as well as rank 8 while moving the answers 6–7× less, and it keeps their accuracy where rank 8 loses about a point. Online (1 seed each), rank 8 had higher AUROC on the paper's evaluation at 6 of 8 checkpoints with ECE split evenly, and drifted about 5× more. No comparison has more than 2 seeds, the learning rate was never retuned per rank, and none was run on the 4-bit model or scored on the full validation set.

1. Offline rank sweep 2 seeds · strongest evidence

bf16 Llama-3-8B, cached base-model answers (8,000 questions), lr 3e-4, answer-KL weight 1, alpha = rank (scale 1), 500 steps. Dev metrics: the trainer's expected-confidence proxy on 508 dev answers.

rankdev Brier (seeds)dev AUROC (seeds)answer KL (nats)regenerated accuracy, start → end

2. Online runs, rank 8 vs rank 4 1 seed each · suggestive

Same settings except rank: online (the model answers its training questions), F1 labels, lr 3e-4, answer-KL weight 1, 2,000 steps on the 8,000-question cache. Rank 8 ran its first 1,000 steps on a Modal L40S and the rest on a RunPod A100; rank 4 ran on an A100 throughout.

3. The paper's evaluation of those online checkpoints 1 seed each · 512 questions

Released evaluation (the model writes answer and confidence; F1 > 0.5; sampled confidence) on the 512 dev questions, every saved adapter. ECE on 512 questions resolves only to about ±0.03.

Accuracy in the same evaluations: rank 8 averages 65.5% over the eight checkpoints, rank 4 66.1%.

What weakens the comparison

  • Seeds. Two per rank offline, one online. Seed-to-seed differences in dev Brier (up to 0.007) are as large as the rank differences.
  • Learning rate. Every rank used 3e-4 with alpha = rank, so each direction gets the same step size. A lower rank might want a higher rate; that was never tested.
  • Hardware and accumulation. Offline, rank 8 ran on a Modal L40S without gradient accumulation and ranks 1–4 on RunPod 4090s with 4-way accumulation. The math is the same, but kernels are nondeterministic, and the base model's regenerated accuracy already differs slightly between the two (0.669 vs 0.667).
  • Model and protocol. All of it is bf16. Our new baseline is the 4-bit model, scored in 4-bit, where neither rank has been run. The online pair was also never scored on the full validation set.
  • Selection. The headline rank-8 checkpoint (step 1,000) was chosen by dev ECE, which flatters it against rank 4's unselected curve.

What would settle it

On the 4-bit model: rank 4 vs rank 8, 3 seeds each, online with F1 labels, 1,000 steps, adapters every 250 steps, scored with the paper's evaluation in 4-bit on the dev questions, and the best checkpoint of each seed on the full validation set. Roughly $5 of RunPod training and $12 of Modal evaluation. Report AUROC, ECE and accuracy per seed, with the spread.
